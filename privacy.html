Privacy Policy

Last updated November 9, 2026

Party Up is a student passion project that helps gamers find teammates. This page explains what information we collect and how we use it, in plain language.

What we collect

  • Login info: when you log in with Google or Discord, we receive your account ID, name and email address. Your email is never shown to other players.
  • Your profile: the gamertag, games, platform, play style, handles (like Discord, PSN or Xbox names), bio and looks you choose to add.
  • Things you post: Looking for Group posts, boss bounties, events, clips, endorsements, favorites and reports.
  • Messages: direct messages and group chats you send on Party Up.
  • Game stats: if you add your Epic Games name, we look up your public Fortnite stats through Fortnite-API.com.

Who can see it

  • Your profile, posts, bounties, events, clips and endorsements are visible to anyone using Party Up.
  • Messages are visible only to the people in that conversation.
  • Favorites and reports are visible only to you.

How we use it

Only to run Party Up: showing your profile to other players, matching you with similar players, and delivering your messages. We don't sell your information, and we don't use it for advertising.

Where it's stored

Party Up's data is stored with Supabase, our database provider. The website is hosted by Netlify. Some settings may also be saved in your own browser.

Deleting your information

You can delete your profile at any time from the Profile page using "Clear my profile". Deleting your profile also removes your posts, bounties, events, clips, endorsements and messages. You can also ask us to delete your account by email.

Kids

Party Up isn't meant for children under 13. If you're under 13, please don't create an account.
